# 06 Brand Guidelines

## Brand Personality
Modern, Confident, Competitive, Inspiring, Student Friendly. Never childish, never boring.

## Colors
- Primary: Deep Red
- Secondary: White
- Background: Very Light Gray
- Cards: Glass White
- Danger: Red, Success: Green, Warning: Orange, Info: Blue

## Typography
- Headings: Bold
- Body: Medium
- Buttons: Semibold

## Icons
Only Lucide Icons. Same stroke width. No mixed icon packs.
